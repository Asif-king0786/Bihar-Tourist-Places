The Top 10 Tourist Places in Bihar

Explore the famous tourist places of Bihar. Click on any link to learn more.

  1. Bodh Gaya

    Famous for the Mahabodhi Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the sacred Bodhi Tree where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment.

    Open Link ↗

  2. Nalanda

    Known for the ancient ruins of Nalanda University, a major centre of learning from the 5th to the 12th century.

    Open Link ↗

  3. Rajgir

    An ancient hill city featuring the Vishwa Shanti Stupa, hot springs, a scenic ropeway, and Venu Van.

    Open Link ↗

  4. Patna

    The state capital featuring the Bihar Museum, Buddha Smriti Park, Patna Zoo, and the sacred Takht Sri Harmandir Sahib Ji Gurdwara.

    Open Link ↗

  5. Vaishali

    An important historical site known for Ashokan Pillars, the Vishwa Shanti Stupa, and its strong ties to Buddhism and Jainism.

    Open Link ↗

  6. Sasaram

    Famous for the magnificent Indo-Afghan-style Tomb of Sher Shah Suri, an impressive architectural marvel.

    Open Link ↗

  7. Gaya

    Renowned for the sacred Vishnupad Temple along the Phalgu River, a major centre for Hindu pind daan ancestor rituals.

    Open Link ↗

  8. Valmiki Tiger Reserve

    Located in West Champaran, it is Bihar's premier wildlife sanctuary and its only tiger reserve.

    Open Link ↗

  9. Pawapuri

    A deeply revered sacred centre for Jains, featuring Jal Mandir, a white marble temple situated in the middle of a lotus pond.

    Open Link ↗

  10. Kesariya

    Home to the Kesariya Stupa, one of the largest ancient Buddhist stupas in the world.

    Open Link ↗
